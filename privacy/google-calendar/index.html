Google Calendar privacy

Updated November 24, 2026

This notice explains the optional Google Calendar connection in Lakewood Commons Calendar, operated by Bais Medrash of Lakewood Commons. Connecting is your choice. You can use the website calendar without connecting Google.

What the connection does

With your Google permission, the service creates a separate Lakewood Commons calendar in your Google account. It copies saved congregation events and keeps their changes, deletions and your chosen Google reminder settings in sync. Changes made in Google do not change the website.

The app requests permission for calendars created by this app. It does not request access to your other calendars or personal appointments. Private admin notes, admin-only events and posted Messages are not exported.

Information we use and store

Google provides authorization tokens and identifiers for the app-created calendar and events. The service stores an encrypted refresh token, the calendar identifier, event synchronization records, reminder preference, connection status, synchronization times and operational errors. These records are associated with your signed-in website account.

The connection sends saved congregation event details, dates, times and locations to Google. Google hosts the resulting calendar and delivers Google reminders according to your account and device settings.

How information is protected and used

The connection runs on Cloudflare. Google refresh tokens and pending authorization codes are encrypted before storage. The website uses HTTPS and does not display your Google tokens in its interface.

Google information is used to operate and support this calendar connection. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or profiling, or use it to train AI models. Google and Cloudflare process the information needed to provide the connection. Connection status and errors may be reviewed for support or security. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, disconnecting and deletion

Connection credentials and synchronization records are retained while the connection is active and while removal is being completed. In the website calendar, open Google Calendar and choose Turn off and remove shul calendar. Once Google confirms removal of that app-created calendar, its stored connection credentials and event synchronization records are deleted. Your personal calendars and the website's shared source events are unchanged.

You can also revoke the app's access in your Google account. Revoking access does not itself remove the Lakewood Commons calendar from Google. If access has expired or been revoked, remove that calendar in Google and use the website's Forget connection option when offered, or contact us for help.

Your choices and questions

You control whether to connect, which supported Google reminder setting to use, and whether to disconnect. Sharing settings you change in Google are governed by Google Calendar.

For questions or a request concerning connection data, email info@baismedrashoflakewoodcommons.org. Identify your website account so we can verify and handle the request. Do not send passwords or Google authorization tokens.
