# TV layout concept

The user-approved direction is retained in all-posters-concept.html. This is a
static review artifact, not the deployed renderer and not production content.
Its photographed dedication and seasonal notices need date confirmation.
The two original photo references are local attachment paths and are deliberately
not bundled as public assets. Use the original conversation attachments to review
those posters. Integrate the layout with DisplayView's authenticated data and
schedule presentation before representing this concept as the live TV design.

Current preferences: David lettering; prayer names and times equally prominent;
labels on the right and times on the left; consistent underlines; gold accents;
Shabbos columns start on the right under one overall heading; Yom Tov has one
overall heading with named/numbered day sections; bottom-center פרנס היום;
donation website QR; all distinct notices available in an independent rotation.
